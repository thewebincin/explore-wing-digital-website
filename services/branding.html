02 Brand Identity

Make your brand feel consistent wherever people meet it.

A brand is more than a logo. We help bring together the visual choices—colour, type, image style and layout—that make your website, social pages and printed materials feel like they belong to the same business.

Identity Package

A visual system your team can use

The work starts with your goals and existing materials. We agree on the deliverables before design begins.

01 Direction

Visual direction

Reference images, audience and business priorities help shape a direction that feels appropriate to your work.

02 Colour

A usable colour palette

Select colours for contrast and consistency, with practical values your designers and printers can reference.

03 Typography

Type that stays readable

Choose a type hierarchy for headings and body copy that works across your main customer-facing materials.

04 Image style

Photography and graphic style

Set a few clear references for photography, illustration or graphic elements so future content feels connected.

05 Applications

Use the identity in context

Apply the agreed system to selected items such as a website, social post or business document.

06 Guidance

A short brand guide

A concise reference can help your team and future suppliers use the colours, type and visual elements consistently.
